The Indus Valley Civilization

Flourishing along the fertile floodplains of the Indus and Saraswati rivers, the Harappan civilization constructed the ancient world's most sophisticated planned urban society. Explore orthogonal avenue grids, covered hydraulic sanitation, standardized decimal metrology, and bustling maritime dockyards.

🏛️ Orthogonal Urban Grid

Avenues intersecting at crisp 90° angles, standardized 1:2:4 burnt bricks, and flood-defying citadel platforms.

🚰 Hydraulic Sanitation

Corbelled subterranean sewers, private terracotta bathrooms, soak pits, and bitumen-waterproofed Great Bath.

⚖️ Weights & Maritime Trade

Standardized binary chert weights (1:2:4:8:16:32), bead factories, and tidal dockyards linked to Sumer & Dilmun.

🦏 Steatite Seals & Script

Intaglio engraved seals of the Unicorn and Pashupati, carrying 400+ undeciphered logosyllabic pictographs.
